PRIVACY POLICY

Your practice belongs to you.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Local-first storage

Still stores your meditation sessions, theme choice, and chime preference locally in your browser using localStorage. Still does not run a central tracking server, analytics, or advertising scripts.

2. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to connect your Google account, Still requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This permission allows Still to create and update its own backup file (Still Meditation Backup.json) in your Google Drive. Still cannot view, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.

  • Your Google OAuth access token is kept in browser memory only and cleared when you sign out or close the tab.
  • Your session history is sent directly from your browser to Google Drive — never to a third-party server.
  • You can revoke Still's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or delete the backup file directly in Drive.

3. Local JSON export

You can export or import your meditation history as a plain JSON file directly on your device without signing in to any third-party account.

4. Ambient audio

All ambient soundscapes and completion chimes are synthesized locally in your browser using the Web Audio API. No microphone access is requested or used.
